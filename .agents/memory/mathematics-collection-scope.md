---
name: Mathematics collection scope
description: Why the worldwide mathematics collection intentionally omits milestones already present in regional history datasets.
---

The user requested: “do not duplicate them and do not move these events into new dataset, just add new events related to mathematics worldwide (I mean whole world - east & west) valuable to the history and the world.”

The mathematics collection supplements the regional collections rather than consolidating them into a self-contained survey. Its omissions of existing mathematical milestones are intentional.

**Why:** The user wanted additional worldwide coverage while preserving the original events and their regional context.

**How to apply:** When extending or revising the mathematics collection, check both tagged events and untagged descriptions in other datasets. Leave existing milestones where they are. Include Eastern as well as Western contributions, with concrete mathematical methods or results rather than biographies alone.
